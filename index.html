WHAT THIS COURSE IS

Not a Video With a Pause Button

Most AI courses sit between two failures: abstract formulas nobody connects to machinery, or library wrappers that hide the machinery entirely. This course rejects both. Every concept is built from first principles, as a mechanism you construct — and the construction is the understanding.

Real algorithms

Byte-pair encoding, backpropagation, Adam, RoPE, attention, SwiGLU, FlashAttention's math — implemented from scratch in your browser, computed live, verified by hand in worked examples. Not approximations labeled as real.

Real interaction

You assemble pipelines, place bricks in loops, build circuits on a bench, and launch missions on a pad. Every widget has a tour state, every lab invites you to break it, and every wrong order fails with a reason.

Real honesty

Toy weights are labeled toy. Approximate numbers say (approx). Failure modes are taught, not hidden. The course never lies to you — that is what makes it trustworthy enough to learn from.

HOW IT WORKS

Why You'll Actually Remember This

Formulas don't stick when they're handed to you. They stick when you build the machine that produces them. Every module runs the same loop.

1 · Predict before reveal

Before any widget shows a computed result, you guess it. "Will SGD or Adam reach the minimum first?" The wrong guess is the memory hook — the reveal lands because you committed first.

2 · Build, don't watch

Every central mechanism is assembled by you: pipelines snap together, loops get their bricks placed, formulas get assembled term by term. Wrong order fails with a reason, not a red X.

3 · Hear the machine

Curves sing. Softmax rows play chords. Loss falls like a descending tone. Sound is a second channel of understanding — and every lab respects the mute button.

4 · Real numbers only

No fake animations. Every score, weight, and gradient on every page is computed live in your browser — from real formulas, on deterministic toy engines that every student shares.

5 · Honest failures

"This model's weights are un-trained placeholders. This router will collapse without its balancing loss. This approximation is labeled." The course never lies to you — that's what makes it trustworthy.

6 · Retrieval at the end

Every module closes with a no-lookup diagnostic exam: five production incidents, five diagnoses, from memory. 4/5 earns a certificate. The difficulty is the point.

FAQ

Straight Answers

+ Is it really "from scratch"? No libraries at all?

Really. No PyTorch, no TensorFlow, no transformers library. Byte-pair encoding, backpropagation, Adam, attention, SwiGLU, FlashAttention's math — all implemented as real algorithms in your browser, with every intermediate value visible. The toy engines use deterministic seeded weights so every student sees the same model.

+ Do I need a GPU or any setup?

Nothing to install, nothing to configure. Open any module in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and it runs — math included. A mid-range laptop handles every lab at 60fps.

+ I'm a beginner. Will Module 00 be too hard?

Module 00 starts with a single AND gate and builds from there — it was designed as the level-setting experience. Every new mechanism gets a fully worked example before you're asked to drive it, and every lab has a tour state you can just watch. The 85% rule applies: if you're failing more than one self-check in five, re-read the section — that's the design working.

+ How is this different from watching Karpathy or reading a blog?

Videos and blogs are consumption — you can't click a formula and watch it compute. Here, every formula is a machine: you predict before it reveals, you assemble pipelines and loops, you patch activations and see outputs move, you hear loss curves fall. The difference is what you can do afterward: rebuild the mechanism from memory, because you built it once with your hands.

+ What do I get at the end?

Fourteen module certificates (4/5 on each diagnostic exam, no looking up) and a final Frontier Engineer certificate for the course. More importantly: the ability to open a blank file and write a working transformer, tokenizer, and training loop from memory — the deliverable stated at the top of this page.

+ Is the content current for 2026?

The core (00–07) teaches mechanisms that don't age — the same blocks Llama, GPT, and Qwen run today. Modules 08–13 cover the 2024–2026 frontier explicitly: test-time compute (o1-class, DeepSeek-R1), hybrid SSMs (Mamba-2, Jamba), MoE at scale (DeepSeek-V3), 1M-token context, and the scaling laws that govern it all. Everything approximate is labeled approximate.
