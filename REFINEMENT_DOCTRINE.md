### R17 - THE BOOK STANDARD: every notebook is a Foundry Manual
1. MINIMUM PAGES: every notebook must have AT LEAST 4 pages. Fewer = the book
   frame has nothing to show; the LEGO page-turn, checkboxes and progress stud
   need a journey to measure.
2. THE ARC (per book): Page 1 ORIGIN (birth of the idea, history, people) ->
   Page 2 MECHANISM (the math/how, worked numbers) -> Page 3 CONSEQUENCE (the
   crash, the win, the application) -> Page 4 THE 2026 LENS (where the idea
   stands TODAY: frontier usage, current research, honest approx labels) ->
   optional Page 5 YOUR MOVE (what the learner builds next; bridge to the next
   module). Every book: at least the 4-page arc.
3. VOICE: the module's own narrative style (history + wit + LEGO metaphor) -
   new pages must read as if they were always there. No lorem, no filler.
4. 2026 HONESTY: frontier claims (model families, capabilities) labeled
   "approx"/"reported" per the course's honesty contract; never invent
   specific false numbers; general direction is fine.
5. FRAME: the Foundry Manual CSS+enhancer (cover plate, parts inventory,
   step bricks + checkboxes, stud-slider progress, staples, ribbon, flip)
   is the single presentation system for ALL modules.
